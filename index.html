Where the talks stand

Owner meetings in Manhattan this week were, in Rob Manfred's words, "almost exclusively" about labor. Both sides are dug in, and the calendar is doing the talking.

Manfred: no deal without a cap and a floor

The commissioner says he "can't imagine" owners ratifying a new CBA without a salary cap and floor, with the Dodgers' back-to-back titles and their payroll looming over the push.

MLB sweetens the money, the union looks elsewhere

MLB raises its minimum-salary offer to $1 million for 2027 — up $220,000 from 2026 — grows the pre-arbitration bonus pool 30% to $65 million, and offers $500,000 bonuses to veterans on cheap one-year deals. The MLBPA counters by pressing for more than $950 million in revenue-sharing help for low-revenue markets and eyes the league's media deals.

Tue, Dec 1 · 11:59 p.m. ET

The CBA expires. Lockout expected at 12:01 a.m.

If there's no deal, owners are expected to lock the players out the moment the agreement lapses — freezing trades, free agency and the entire offseason.

What it means

If the doors close at 12:01 a.m.

A lockout isn't a strike. It's the owners closing the shop — and it freezes everything fans care about in the offseason.

🚫

Trades and free agency stop

No signings, no trades, no deals. The hot stove goes cold the same week the Winter Meetings would have been the center of the baseball world.

🔒

Player activity halts

Players can't use team facilities, talk to club staff, or work out under team supervision. Communication between the sides freezes.

📅

The clock eats the calendar

The 2021 lockout lasted 99 days and pushed back Opening Day. A longer one could threaten the 2027 season itself.

⚖️

One issue sits under all of it

A salary cap and floor. The last time owners formally proposed one, in 1994, the players struck for more than seven months and the World Series was canceled.

The two sides

MLB's offer vs. the union's answer

As of November 23, 2026.

MLB / THE OWNERS
  • Salary cap and floor — the centerpiece, and the line Manfred won't cross.
  • Minimum salary to $1 million in 2027, up $220,000 from 2026.
  • Pre-arbitration bonus pool up 30%, from $50 million to $65 million.
  • $500,000 bonuses for veterans signing one-year deals of $10 million or less.
  • Free agency at five years for players 30 and older.
THE MLBPA / THE PLAYERS
  • No salary cap. The union's opposition stretches back decades.
  • More than $950 million in revenue-sharing assistance to low-revenue markets, up from roughly $700 million.
  • $20 million rewards maintained for revenue-sharing teams that make the playoffs or outplay the worst playoff team.
  • Eyes the media deals — the union is pressing on where the league's money comes from.
  • Points to the record: 93% of players settled arbitration without a hearing under the current CBA.
